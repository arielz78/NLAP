Local Aroma

Today’s Theme: 10 Local Legends

  1. 🍝 Funghhetto Trattoria – Woodbridge Elegance

    A polished Italian dining room known for rich flavours, refined plates, and warm hospitality.
    💡 Tip: Try the lamb, mushroom risotto, or anything with truffle - they never miss.
    Try Tonight

  1. 🍷 Zafferano Ristorante – Weston Road Classic

    Family-run for over 20 years, serving comforting Italian flavours in a relaxed, elegant setting.
    💡 Tip: Their veal marsala and house-made pastas are local favourites.
    Book a Table

  1. 🍽️ Tovico Trattoria – Woodbridge Favourite

    A welcoming spot where beautifully presented Italian dishes meet heartfelt service and generous portions.
    💡 Tip: Order the gnocchi or the signature pizzas, both are crowd-pleasers.

    Taste it

  1. 🍇 Avenue Cibi e Vini – Kleinburg Icon

    A fashionable Italian favourite built on wood-fired pizza, elevated mains, and a fantastic wine list.
    💡 Tip: Ask for the pasta vongole — it’s a regular’s go-to.
    Plan Your Visit

  1. ☕ Kleinburg Kafé – Village Staple

    A cozy all-day favourite known for great cappuccinos, simple comfort dishes, and warm service.
    💡 Tip: Their pastries go fast — especially the tiramisu cups.
    Enjoy
